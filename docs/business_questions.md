# Business Questions

The purpose of this project is to answer the following business questions:

## Revenue

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which regions contribute the highest sales?
- Which customer segment is the most valuable?

## Profitability

- Which products have the highest profit margin?
- Are discounts reducing profitability?

## Trends

- How does revenue change month by month?
- Are there seasonal patterns?

## Business Opportunities

- Which products deserve more investment?
- Which regions require additional attention?
